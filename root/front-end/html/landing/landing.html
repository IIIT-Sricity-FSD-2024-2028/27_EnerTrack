What We Do

Not software you install. A partner you hire.

Most institutions already know they waste energy. What they lack is a verified baseline, someone qualified to find the savings, and a way to prove the savings actually happened.

EnerTrack provides all three. Our certified energy auditors visit your site and establish an evidence-backed baseline. Our platform then tracks consumption against it and flags variance. Your own facilities team carries out the fixes, because they know your buildings better than we do.

We only take a performance share when the savings are measured and agreed, so we are paid for outcomes rather than activity.

How An Engagement Works

From first site visit to verified savings.

Every client follows the same six stages. Stage one is a fixed fee. Everything after that is tied to what the platform can actually measure.

01

Audit and site visit

A certified auditor walks your campus, records what metering you already have, and identifies where the savings are.

02

Contract

Subscription tier and performance-share terms are agreed up front, so there are no surprises when the first savings invoice arrives.

03

Baseline and setup

Your organisation is provisioned, your buildings and meters are registered, and the verified baseline is locked in.

04

Ongoing monitoring

Dashboards, reports and variance alerts run continuously. Your facilities team is notified when consumption drifts from baseline.

05

Recommendation tracking

We issue the recommendations. Your team implements them and marks progress, so nothing is claimed that was not actually done.

06

Savings verification

Post-implementation consumption is compared against baseline. The agreed share is invoiced only on what is measured.

Commercial Model

You pay for outcomes, not licences.

Three components. The first is fixed, the second scales with the size of your estate, and the third only applies when savings are proven.

Audit fee

One-time, paid up front

Covers the certified auditor and field team for the site assessment and baseline establishment. A share is credited back against your first year of subscription.

Monitoring subscription

Recurring, priced per meter

Ongoing access to dashboards, reporting, variance alerts and account management. Priced by the size of your metered estate rather than by seat, so adding colleagues costs nothing.

Performance share

Only on verified savings

A minority share of savings measured against your baseline, payable only where recommendations were implemented and the reduction can be evidenced.

Pricing depends on estate size and existing metering. Your audit proposal includes projected savings and an estimated payback period before you commit to anything.
